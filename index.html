The Bloom Project is a 501(c)(3) social impact creative studio. Mission

Cultivating joy, one moment at a time

We believe joy is a catalyst, so we create it on purpose — through a smile to a stranger, a warm coffee with a friend, a vibrant mural by local artists, or a shirt that sparks a new conversation. We are doing this work because when we needed it, our communities wrapped their arms around us. And the most beautiful way we know how to say thank you is to pass that grace forward.

The Bloom Project took root in Monona, WI, on MLK Day in 2026, brought to life by a small circle of first-generation immigrant women, mothers, and friends. It started simply, with just three of us — Marina D C Zalewski, Luiza Paster, and Jocelyn Tian — and the life-changing power of two phrases: "já deu certo" and "whatever you need."

We didn't wait for the perfect time; we just decided to leave our corners of the world a little better than we found them, starting exactly where we were, with exactly what we had, together. And now, we invite everyone to join us in planting seeds of joy — welcome to The Bloom Project.

Project No. 1 · our first

From Seed to Bloom

The 825 E Johnson mural, a collaboration with DAMA, Developing Artist Murals and Alliances.

The project we launched with. Under the guidance of local artist Emily Rodriguez at DAMA headquarters, seventeen young people aged 14–17 earned a paid wage ($15/hr) to learn, design, and paint alongside the pros. Together they created a public mural that blossoms, panel by panel, from seed to bloom — so much more than paint. It is about equipping kids with real-world skills, teamwork, and the confidence to make their own corners of the world a little sweeter.
