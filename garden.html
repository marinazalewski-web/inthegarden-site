Welcome to the Garden

You're already someone's reason to bloom.

The Garden is where The Bloom Project's idea keeps growing between projects — a place to practice the golden rule on purpose, together. Right now, that means backing a real mural going up on Madison's east side, painted by the kids who live there. Everywhere else, it means the same simple thing: reach toward your neighbor, and grow something together.

What joining actually means

A commitment to living by the golden rule is all it takes — it gets you into our members database and, soon, our WhatsApp group, where the Garden actually happens. Add a donation of any kind, and we'll send a welcome pack your way. No minimum, no belief test — just the intention, shared.

Living by the golden rule

Simply: treat others the way you'd want to be treated — starting with yourself, so it has somewhere to grow from. In practice, that looks like:

  • Loving yourself first, so you have something left to give
  • Looking the next person in the eye like they matter — because they do
  • Showing up in small, ordinary ways: a nod, a meal, a ride, a listening ear
  • Standing up for others the way you'd want someone to stand up for you

That's it. No perfect track record required — just the intention, practiced on purpose, starting today.
